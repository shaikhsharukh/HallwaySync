Frequently Asked Questions

How do attendees join a HallwaySync room?

Attendees point their native camera at the stage projector screen to open the web room immediately. No app store installation or password signup is required.

Is participant contact information private?

Yes. Contact handles (Telegram, LinkedIn, GitHub) are strictly hidden until two attendees mutually send connection waves or scan physical badge QR codes.

What features are available for hackathon organizers?

Organizers get an operational command center with real-time demographic charts, live attendee moderation, broadcast stage announcements, and CSV export.
